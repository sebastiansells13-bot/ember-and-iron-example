About the Shop

Ember & Iron is a one-person forge — no showroom, no sales team, just a shop with a coal forge, an anvil that's older than the building around it, and a backlog of custom orders.

Most work falls into three buckets: architectural hardware (gates, railings, hinges), knives, and repair work on ironwork that's usually older than I am. If it's steel and it's broken, bent, or just doesn't exist yet, that's the job.

Turnaround varies a lot by piece — the order estimator gives a rough sense of both price and lead time before you reach out with the real specifics.
